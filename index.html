What we build

Software that runs where
your business runs.

Multi-vendor spaces, multi-location shops, and hospitality operations have problems generic software doesn't solve. CoreRail builds the specific tools that do.

Vendor settlements

Weekly settlements pulled from live Square POS data. Commission splits, delivery fees, daily minimums, linen charges, balance carryover — calculated, not guessed. CPA-ready Excel exports and full audit logging.

Shift & inventory ops

Schedules with swaps and time-off, opening and closing checklists, bar inventory with par-level alerts, team messaging with ack-required announcements, real-time labor cost monitoring. One installable web app on every staff phone — opens like a native app, no App Store, no IT install.

Web presence & identity

Professional websites with vendor pages, event listings, online ordering integration, schema markup, and the kind of web presence that matches the quality of the space behind it.
