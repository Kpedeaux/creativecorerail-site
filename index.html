Questions operators ask me

What does it cost?

The Coffee Shop Portal runs $149 to $559 a month by locations and team size, with a 14-day free trial. Sites & Marketing is $499 a month. Both together are $599 a month at any portal tier, even Pro. Custom one-off projects get a written proposal with the scope, timeline, and price in one place.

Do I subscribe, or do I own it?

The two products are subscriptions, because they have to stay running, updated, and supported. Anything custom I build outside them, like a standalone website project, is yours outright: the code, the domain, the content.

My records are a mess. Is that a problem?

No. Messy is normal. Send your schedule, recipes, suppliers, and counts in any form they exist and I load everything myself, free. You get a working system on day one.

Aren't you busy running your own businesses?

Yes, and that's the point. The software gets tested against a real service rush every day before it ever gets to you. When you call, you get the person who builds it and uses it.

Does it work with Square?

That's the core of it. CoreRail is built on the Square platform: live sales data, labor, loyalty, and payments. If you're on Square, most of the wiring already exists.

Tell me what's broken.

Every system I build starts with a problem an operator is already living with. Bring yours.
