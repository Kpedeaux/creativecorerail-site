Privacy

Last updated July 31, 2026

I'm Kevin, and creativecorerail.com is my shop window. This page describes everything the site collects, which is not much.

The contact form

When you send the form, your name, email, and message go through a Cloudflare Worker straight to my inbox, delivered by Resend, an email service. The message also carries the country and city Cloudflare saw your request come from and your browser type, which helps me spot spam. Nothing is stored on the site itself: the form's only job is to become an email to me. I use what you send to reply, and for nothing else.

No cookies, no analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ad tracking. Fonts are self-hosted, so pages load nothing from third parties.

The free tools

The photo editor and word processor run entirely in your browser. Files you open never leave your device. The word processor autosaves your document to your own browser's local storage so you don't lose work; it never sends it anywhere.

Your choices

Email pedeaux@gmail.com and I will tell you what I have about you (at most, an email thread), correct it, or delete it. Some browsers send Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals; the site does not change what it collects when it sees them, because this page already describes everything it collects.

CoreRail, the product

The CoreRail platform itself (the software my customers sign into) has its own privacy policy and terms.
