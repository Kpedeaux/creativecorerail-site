Multi-Location Coffee Operations

Give your managers visibility.
Give your baristas clarity.

The Barista Portal is a full operations platform built for CR Coffee Roasters — three shops across New Orleans (Magazine Street, Metairie Road, and St. Roch Market). Staff scheduling with shift swaps and time-off requests, internal messaging, shift checklists, inventory tracking, recipes, and project management — all in one PWA.

Launch App

The Problem

You run three coffee shops. Each location has its own opening routine, its own supplier relationships, its own inventory rhythm. Your lead barista at Magazine doesn't know what Metairie is low on. Schedules live in a spreadsheet. Shift swap requests come through text messages. New hires don't know the recipes. And you're managing it all through group texts and shared Google Docs.

The Solution

Barista Portal gives each location its own workspace with a full scheduling system, internal messaging, shift checklists, inventory counts, and notes. Staff set their availability and request time off in the app. Managers publish schedules, approve swaps, and message the team — all without leaving the platform. Leads count inventory against par levels. Baristas pull up recipes on shift. Owners track projects across all three stores from one login.

Staff Scheduling

Full scheduling system with weekly schedule creation, reusable templates, and week-to-week copying. Staff set recurring availability and date-specific overrides. Time-off requests with manager approval workflow. Shift swap requests with accept, counter-offer, and manager review. Total hours tracking per person per week.

Internal Messaging

Built-in messaging with direct messages, location group chats, managers-only channels, and all-staff announcements. Pinned messages for important updates. Read receipts and message acknowledgments for required reads. Auto-refresh every 10 seconds. No more group texts.

Push Notifications

PWA with Web Push API integration. Instant notifications for published schedules, time-off decisions, shift swap updates, and new messages. Installable on any device — works like a native app from the home screen.

Location-Specific Checklists

Opening, mid-shift, and closing task lists customized per location. Each item tracks completion by user and date. Progress bars show shift completion. Admin-editable structure with 50+ seeded tasks per location.

Inventory & Supplier Management

Quick-count and full-count modes. Products tracked with par levels, unit types, prices, item numbers, and brands. Below-par alerts flag what needs ordering. 20+ suppliers with contact info, order guides, and deadlines. Session merging for multi-staff counts.

Recipes & Barista Binder

Searchable recipe library organized by category (Coffee, Milk Drinks, Specialty Drinks). Expandable cards with ingredients and step-by-step instructions. Barista Binder sections for Cash Handling, Daily Expectations, and FAQ.

Shift Notes & Search

Date, shift, and location-specific notes with manager attribution. Recent notes display for shift-to-shift handoff. Full-text search across all notes with date, shift type, location, and user filtering. Last 30 notes archive with expandable details.

Projects & Task Management

Projects with priority levels (Low, Medium, High, Urgent). Assign to specific locations or all locations. Status tracking with completion notes. Color-coded priority badges. Admin completion and reopen controls.

Admin Panel

User management with role assignment (Barista, Manager/Lead, Owner/Admin). Editable checklists per location and shift type. Push notification configuration and testing. Password resets and account creation.

Built for Your Team

Every role in your coffee shops has access to what they need.

Barista

Access checklists, shift notes, recipes, and the barista binder. Pull up procedure cards during service. See what the last shift noted. Stay aligned without asking.

Lead

Everything baristas have, plus full inventory management. Count and track par levels. See what's below par and view supplier contact info to order right away. Merge inventory sessions with other leads. Flag items for owner review.

Owner

Everything plus product and supplier management. Create and edit products, suppliers, checklists, and recipes. Manage projects across all locations. Create accounts and assign user roles. Set pricing and par levels. Export reports.
